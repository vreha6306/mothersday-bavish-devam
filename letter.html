To The Most Beautiful Soul 💖

Dear Mom,
No words will ever be enough to thank you for all the love, care, and sacrifices you’ve made for me. You were the one who held my hand when I was learning to walk, and even today, you are the one who gives me strength whenever life feels difficult. Your love is the safest place I have ever known, and your smile is still my biggest comfort. No matter how much I grow, a part of me will always remain your little child who needs your warmth, your hugs, and your presence. Thank you for loving me even on the days when I was hardest to handle.
If I become even half the person you are, I will consider my life successful.
I love you forever, Maa 💖
